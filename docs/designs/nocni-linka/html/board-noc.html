Noční linka

For the person who opens the site at two in the morning. The site is dark, quiet and very large: on desktop a fixed side rail holds the name, the nonstop number and the navigation, and the content scrolls beside it as a sequence of single statements, one per screen. Nothing competes with the number, and nothing has to be read twice.

Density
very sparse; one statement per screen
Page flow
fixed rail + scrolling statements (desktop); number first, then statements (mobile)
Colour
dark ground, warm off-white text, amber only for the nonstop number
Photos
none; type only. A photo on O nás at most, never on home
Navigation
vertical list in the rail (desktop); “Obsah” full-page list (mobile)
Emergency
the rail itself; on mobile a dark panel directly under the name
Notices
large-type agenda, one ceremony per row, date as the heading
