Plný přehled

For families who want facts, not atmosphere: the practical son or daughter who arranges everything and compares. The home page is a directory, like a timetable or a municipal noticeboard: every route, the key prices, the people and the documents fit on one desktop screen, in four ruled columns. Nobody has to scroll or guess what is behind a menu; on a phone the same index folds into a list of sections.

Density
dense; the whole home fits one 1280 × 800 screen
Page flow
no story: a directory home; every page is a reference sheet with tables
Colour
white and black; one highlighter colour behind the nonstop number only
Photos
small catalogue thumbnails: people, coffins, flowers; never decorative
Navigation
every page in one full nav row (1280); “Rejstřík” and folding sections (375)
Emergency
highlighted box in the header; office hours in a separate box beside it
Notices
table: date · time · name · place · type, upcoming first
