Rodinný rozhovor

For a small family firm that wants the site to sound like the first phone call with them: warm, direct, human. The page is a conversation: every section is a question the family is asking (“Kdo nám pomůže hned?”, “Co nás teď čeká?”, “Kolik to bude stát?”) with a short plain answer, and the answer to the first question is the phone number. Warm colour fields and hand-drawn line illustrations replace photographs, so it is gentle without being sombre.

Density
medium; one question per section, answers of two or three sentences
Page flow
question and answer; full-bleed fields alternate cream and sand; the first is deep clay
Colour
warm cream, sand and deep clay; or honey with deep olive
Photos
commissioned pen drawings (local trees, the square, the office door) in the margins; one real photo of the people under “Kdo jsme”
Navigation
always visible words, wrapping on mobile; no menu button, no JS at all
Emergency
the answer to the first question, in the clay field; repeated as the last sentence of every page
Notices
notice pages answer “Kdy a kde”, “Na dálku”, “Kondolence”; no drawings, no fields
